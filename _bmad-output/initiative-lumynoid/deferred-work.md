  summary: The `task_running` FGS notification is invisible on Android 13+ until the app declares and asks for `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`; hand that to epic-tugas-terjadwal, which owns the notification permission.

The owner chose to accept this and leave notifications to epic-tugas-terjadwal instead of declaring the permission now.
